# Section 6: The Workflow

## Slide 25 — The Four Phases

**Diagram (TikZ):**

```
+----------+     +-----------+     +--------+     +----------+
| Explore  | --> |  Propose  | --> | Apply  | --> | Archive  |
+----------+     +-----------+     +--------+     +----------+
  Think           Formalize        Implement       Close &
  Investigate     Plan             Verify          Update Specs
```

A full cycle from idea to done.

---

## Slide 26 — Explore

**`/opsx:explore`**

- Investigate the codebase
- Ask questions, think freely
- No commitments, no files written
- Pure discovery

"Thinking time, not task time."

---

## Slide 27 — Propose

**`/opsx:propose add-dark-mode`**

- Creates change folder with all artifacts
- AI drafts proposal, design, specs, tasks
- Human reviews and approves each artifact

Nothing is built until YOU say so.

---

## Slide 28 — Apply

**`/opsx:apply`**

- Implements tasks one by one
- Each task verified before moving to next
- AI follows the design, not its imagination

The spec is the guardrail.

---

## Slide 29 — Archive

**`/opsx:archive`**

- Change is completed and archived
- Main specs updated with new requirements
- History preserved for future reference

The loop closes. Specs are the living record.
